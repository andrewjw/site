Recently I made my now annual trek into London for LDX3, the festival for engineering leadership. For the second time
this was held at the [Intercontinental at the O2](https://iclondon-theo2.com/). LDX3 is a huge conference with over
2,600 attendees, three main stages, two smaller "solutions" stages, a wide range of table talks and community meet ups,

<!--more-->

This year some changes had been made which addressed most of the concerns I had - food was plentiful for lunch and all
the breaks, and the stages in general were better. The two smaller tracks - Making Technical Choices and Building For
Production - had moved downstairs where there was more room. These rooms were not perfect, and the Technical Choices
room was very wide and not very deep, which meant you could be a long way off to the side of the stage. The biggest
issues on these stages was that the screens went from floor to ceiling, and given the seating was not raked mean you
couldn't see the bottom half of the screens. LeadDev obviously heard this, and for day two the slides were shrunk and
moved up so it was much easier to see.

![Hard To Read Screen](/assets/ldx3_london_2026_blocked_screen.jpeg)

The Intercontinental is not the easiest to get to (especially for those of us coming from north of London), and is a bit
soulless, but after the teething troubles of last year seems to work well. While I'm happy to see LeadDev be successful,
I kind of hope LDX3 doesn't grow much further as it's already so big, and there is so much going. It's fairly overwhelming
during the breaks.

One change from previous years is that the subtitles were AI generated, instead of being live transcribed by a human. 
I'm lucky enough to not need them, but I do use them occasionally to check back on what was said while writing my
notes. The AI subtitles were mostly fine, but definitely struggled on occasions - particular if the speaker was not
a native English speaker. I also missed the human touch of the stage host interacting with the transcriber at the start
of the day. Maybe I'm just old school though.

In between the talks whoever playlists the music between talks knows exactly what kind of music I like](https://www.last.fm/user/andrew_j_w).
La Roux, Kurt Vile, Field Music and many more. I would absolutely go to a LeadDev DJ gig - it'd be wall to wall bangers!
